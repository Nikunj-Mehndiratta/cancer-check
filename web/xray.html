Chest X-ray research model

Upload a frontal chest X-ray (PNG, JPEG or DICOM). An open-source research model scores it for 18 findings, including the three linked to lung cancer: mass, nodule and lung lesion. A heatmap shows which parts of the image drove each score.

Read this first. This model is not approved for medical use and is not a radiologist. It can miss cancers and flag things that aren't there. It has no "cancer" output: a mass or nodule can be a tumour, but most are scars or old infections. It only understands frontal chest X-rays. It doesn't reject other grey images, and will give meaningless scores for MRIs, CT slices, side-view X-rays or photos of a screen. Always discuss real scans with your doctor.

Have symptoms too? Start the symptom checklist. Its results page has this same X-ray step, so you get one combined report.

Model: TorchXRayVision DenseNet-121 (densenet121-res224-all), trained on 8 public chest X-ray datasets. Project page. Scores are rescaled so 0.5 is the model's flagging threshold; they are not probabilities of disease.
